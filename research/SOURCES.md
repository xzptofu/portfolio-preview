# Research figure sources

The online-learning chart reproduces Figure 3(b), page 9, from Zhipan Xu and
Lijun Zhang, *Online Non-convex Learning in Dynamic Environments* (NeurIPS 2024).
The source is the [published paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/5d1233f819202ade06023346df80a6d2-Paper-Conference.pdf).
The curves and uncertainty bands were extracted from the PDF's vector paths;
colors and presentation were adapted for the site. The scripts in `scripts/`
regenerate and check those paths against the source PDF.

The PyNOL architecture illustration comes from the
[PyNOL repository](https://github.com/xzptofu/PyNOL/blob/xzp/docs/_static/figures/PyNOL_structure.png).
The MIT license is included in `PyNOL-LICENSE.txt`.
